Side Bet Offerings: Super 6, Dragon Bonus, and Pair Multipliers

While the standard Player (1:1 payout) and Banker (0.95:1 payout after 5% commission) bets carry the lowest house edge in baccarat, side bets introduce higher payout multipliers for players willing to accept increased volatility. Both providers feature an extensive suite of side wagers alongside alternative game rules.

No Commission Baccarat and Super 6 Mechanics

Standard baccarat deducts a 5% commission on winning Banker wagers to account for the Banker hand’s mathematical advantage. Both SA Gaming and Dream Gaming offer No Commission baccarat tables (often called Punto Banco or Super 6 tables) where winning Banker bets pay a flat 1:1. However, to maintain the casino’s mathematical edge, a winning Banker hand with a total of exactly 6 points pays out at 1:2 (50% of the original wager).

To offset this potential reduction, both studios provide the Super 6 side bet. If the Banker wins with a 6, the Super 6 wager pays out at 12:1. On certain specialised tables, if the Banker wins with a 6 consisting of three cards, the payout can escalate to 18:1 or 20:1 depending on published house paytables. This mode eliminates the need to calculate odd commission payouts in ringgit amounts, speeding up hand settlements.

Pair Wagers and the Dragon Bonus Scale

Beyond Super 6, both providers feature classic pair bets and point-spread multipliers:

  • Player Pair and Banker Pair: Pays 11:1 if the initial two cards dealt to the chosen side form a matching pair (such as two Kings or two Sevens).
  • Perfect Pair: Pays 25:1 or higher if the initial two cards on either side match in both rank and suit (for example, two Jacks of Spades).
  • Dragon Bonus (Natural and Non-Natural Spread): A progressive side bet that pays 1:1 on natural wins (initial two-card 8 or 9) and scales up to 30:1 on non-natural wins determined by the point differential. A four-point margin pays 1:1, whereas a nine-point blowout victory (9 points against 0) yields the maximum 30:1 return.
  • Big and Small Bets: Available on select SA Gaming tables, wagering on whether the total number of cards dealt in the round will be four (Small, paying 1.5:1) or five to six (Big, paying 0.54:1).
